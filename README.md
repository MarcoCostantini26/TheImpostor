# The Impostor
A social deduction game where players identify an impostor through debate.

## Game Rules
- A group of players (_crewmates_) receives a secret word (the same for everyone) except one of them (_impostor_), who receives a clue.
- None of the crewmates know the impostor's identity.
- In turn, each player submits a word that can be somehow connected to the secret word.
- The crewmates' goal is to clear themselves, but at the same time not let the impostor know the secret word and catch him.
- The impostor's goal is to blend in with the crewmates, possibly trying to figure out the secret word.
- At the end of each round, there is a discussion phase, during which each player can express their doubts/suspicions via chat.
- Following the discussion, each player can vote on who he thinks is the impostor.
- **Impostor - Win conditions**:
  - If the impostor is caught but he finally guesses the secret word, he wins.
  - If the impostor manages to perfectly deceive the other players, making them all eliminate and remaining 1vs1 with a crewmate, he wins.
- **Crewmates - Win condition**:
  - If the crewmates manage to catch the impostor and he fails to guess the secret word, the crewmates win.
